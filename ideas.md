# Portal Orbita — Delete Account Page
## Style Decisions

**Design Direction:** Dark Celestial Utility
- Deep navy/black surfaces (#070b14 base)
- Warm solar-gold brand accents (amber-400/orange-500) for identity, emphasis, and navigation
- Red reserved only for irreversible/destructive deletion actions and critical warnings
- Restrained orbit/star-map geometry used as recurring signature motif
- Copy voice: calm, transparent, privacy-first, with subtle orbital/celestial warmth
- Typography: Space Grotesk for display, Inter for body — strong weight contrast
